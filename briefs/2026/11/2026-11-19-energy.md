### Power-sector reform and tariffs

In Ghana, PURC is the primary starting point for tariff decisions and regulatory publications; the Energy Commission is a key source for licensing and planning. Verify the effective date, customer class, tariff components and applicable units directly in the underlying instrument.

For any grid-connected proposal, seek written connection and offtake terms. A general system expansion plan does not establish a project’s connection capacity, delivery date or curtailment protection.

### Storage and off-grid
No new battery-storage award or pay-as-you-go solar initiative is independently confirmed for today. The actionable diligence questions remain: who owns and dispatches the battery; what duration and use case are specified; how are degradation, replacement, warranty and recycling handled; and who pays, on what schedule and in which currency?

For off-grid and PAYGo models, test affordability and repayment performance at household or productive-use level. Distribution, servicing and collections often matter as much as panel cost; mobile-money collection may help where locally supported, but does not remove credit, affordability or service risks.

## Commercial Opportunity

**Best near-term angle: contracted commercial and industrial solar-plus-storage, paired with local integration and after-sales capability.** Start with a narrow customer segment whose outage or fuel costs can be measured—such as cold storage, processing, hospitality or retail—and build the proposition from interval load data, site survey and an auditable baseline. Offer a staged design: efficiency and controls first, then solar, then storage sized to a defined reliability or peak-management use case.

Revenue can come from EPC and recurring O&M, or a third-party-owned energy service where the customer’s credit quality and contract permit it. Do not call a project bankable until the customer’s payment history, tariff class, grid connection, FX exposure, equipment warranty, insurance and payment-security arrangements have been tested. A local service network and transparent performance reporting can be a more defensible entry point than speculative ownership of generation assets.

## Watch List

- Official procurement portals and project sponsor notices for tender scope, deadlines, award status and financing conditions.
- PURC tariff decisions and Energy Commission licensing/planning publications in Ghana; equivalent regulator and ministry notices in other target markets.
- Grid connection, transmission/distribution works and written curtailment or dispatch provisions for proposed projects.
- Storage procurement specifications: MW versus MWh, duration, ownership, dispatch rights, degradation guarantees and end-of-life obligations.
- Actual financing close and disbursement, rather than headline announcements or programme ceilings.
