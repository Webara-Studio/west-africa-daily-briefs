## Commercial Opportunity

The sharpest opportunity is **trusted cross-border access infrastructure**, not simply chasing a headline IPO. A Ghana- or Nigeria-based broker, fintech or custodian could pilot a regulated diaspora investment channel that connects overseas investors to local listed securities and primary offers. Start with one corridor (UK–Ghana is a practical test), a licensed broker/custody partner and a narrow instrument set; make fees, FX conversion, settlement times, tax treatment, eligibility, KYC/AML and risk disclosures explicit. Revenue could come from transparent transaction, custody and issuer-distribution fees. The NGX–NSE stake adds strategic relevance, but do not promise seamless regional trading until exchange and regulator arrangements are confirmed. Measure funded accounts, repeat investment, failed settlement rate and fully loaded acquisition cost before expanding.

A second, less capital-intensive angle is issuer readiness: audited reporting, governance, valuation preparation and investor-relations support for mid-sized West African businesses that are not yet public-market ready. The scarcity is often credible, investable supply and trust as much as investor demand.
