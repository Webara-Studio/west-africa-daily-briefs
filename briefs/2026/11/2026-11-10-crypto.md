## Commercial Opportunity
The strongest near-term play is not launching another speculative token. It is a compliant, corridor-specific settlement and reconciliation service for remittance operators, fintechs or merchants: quote the customer in GBP/USD, convert through a permitted liquidity partner, pay out in GHS/NGN through bank or MoMo rails, and provide auditable transaction records. Start with one corridor and one licensed partner; measure delivered payout versus incumbent, all-in FX/fee, settlement time, failed payouts, prefunding and compliance cost. Do not custody customer assets or market returns without legal review and the appropriate authorisation.

A second, lower-capital angle is compliance and treasury infrastructure for VASPs: customer screening, transaction monitoring, travel-rule workflows where applicable, reserve/redemption evidence and daily reconciliation. Ghana's reported rules and Nigeria's reported capital thresholds make partner readiness more commercially urgent—but verify the actual legal texts before product commitments.

## Watch List
- **Ghana BoG stablecoin directive:** obtain the original notice, covered entities, transition dates and permitted use cases before pricing a product.
- **Nigeria CBN circular:** validate reported capital amounts, issuer definitions and implementation timeline; potential market-entry economics could change sharply.
- **Busha NGNb:** confirm live status, reserve attestations, redemption channels and regulatory permissions; a launch headline alone is insufficient.
- **Circle's Africa expansion and Yellow Card:** look for named markets, licensed partners and disclosed payment/settlement metrics, not hiring announcements alone.
- **e-Cedi and corridor data:** watch for a dated pilot result and credible Ghana/UK or Nigeria/US all-in cost and payout-time studies.
