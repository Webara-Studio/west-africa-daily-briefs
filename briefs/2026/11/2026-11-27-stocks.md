## Commercial Opportunity

**The strongest opportunity is a compliant diaspora investment access and intelligence layer, not an unlicensed trading app.** Start asset-light: partner with licensed brokers/custodians in selected markets, provide issuer and market research, portfolio reporting in both local currency and the investor's base currency, and clear explanations of fees, liquidity and FX risk. Pilot one corridor—such as UK-based diaspora investors accessing Ghanaian listed securities—before expanding.

Revenue could come from research subscriptions, issuer-paid investor-relations services or disclosed referral arrangements with licensed firms. The critical gates are regulatory perimeter analysis, custody and settlement reliability, cross-border marketing rules, and a credible process for handling complaints and suitability. Validate paid demand with a small research-and-education pilot before building transaction functionality.

A complementary B2B opportunity is helping well-governed Ghanaian and regional businesses prepare for capital raising: audited reporting, governance, investor materials and readiness assessment. This is commercially more immediate than assuming a pipeline of imminent IPOs.

## Watch List

- Daily official exchange bulletins and regulator notices for session prices, turnover, suspensions, corporate actions and admission updates.
- Formal offer documents and status milestones—regulatory filing, approval, subscription opening, pricing, allotment and first trading day—before describing any IPO as active or completed.
- Currency and rates: compare local-market returns with FX-adjusted outcomes, particularly for diaspora investors.
- Evidence of liquidity: turnover, free float, bid–ask spreads and concentration in the largest constituents.
- Product launches aimed at diaspora investors: verify provider authorisation, custody arrangements, fees, redemption terms and cross-border compliance.
