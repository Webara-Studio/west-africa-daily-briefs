### Production, exports and processing

MyJoyOnline reports NADKANSCO commissioned its Tuobodom plant on 17 November with installed raw-cashew capacity of 100 metric tonnes/day. The report says the firm exported about 39,700 tonnes of raw nuts during 2022–24 and has a registered outgrower network of about 6,000 farmers. These are company-reported historical figures, not current-season export totals. The reported farmer programme, aggregation/quality centres and boreholes could address supply consistency, but the factory's procurement volumes, run-rate and export shipments still need independent tracking. ### Investment and partnerships

A second Ghana development is a reported memorandum between Jospong subsidiary African Agribusiness Consortium and Türkiye-based ChitoGlobal Biotechnology to conduct Ghana farm trials of products derived from shrimp/crab-shell chitosan. The announced next steps are research, field trials and possible technology transfer—not commercial production. Evidence gates are local regulatory and standards clearance, replicated efficacy, cost per hectare, raw-material availability and buyer willingness to pay.

### Policy, subsidies and climate

Treat this as a forecast rather than a subsidy announcement or assured local price cut. The World Bank warns of upside risks from Middle East shipping disruption and export restrictions. ## Commercial Opportunity

**Best near-term angle: cashew procurement and quality infrastructure around Bono East, rather than speculative greenfield factory construction.** The newly commissioned plant supplies a credible prospective offtaker, while the reported outgrower and aggregation plans signal procurement demand. A practical 60–90 day pilot: secure a written intake specification and payment schedule from the processor; aggregate a small, traceable lot through farmer groups; test moisture/defect and kernel recovery; log farmgate-to-factory transport, rejection rate and cash conversion cycle. Scale only against signed offtake and verified plant intake. Separately, bio-input trials may offer an attractive import-substitution route, but only after regulatory approval and multi-site yield/cost evidence.

## Watch List

- Pass-through from forecast global fertiliser easing to Ghana landed costs, distributor inventory and farm-retail prices.
- Jospong–ChitoGlobal trial protocol, regulatory approvals and published field results; an MoU alone is not commercial validation.
