## Commercial Opportunity

**Best risk-adjusted angle: buyer-led aggregation and quality assurance for cashew and shea, with processing kept modular.** Build a small network that aggregates from known producer groups, measures moisture and grade at collection, consolidates lots, and sells against written buyer specifications. Start with a committed offtaker or paid sample order; only then scale collection or invest in equipment. This lowers exposure to inventory-price swings and turns local trust, consistent quality and traceability into the margin.

A second angle is cocoa services rather than speculative stock: farm rehabilitation, input distribution tied to transparent repayment/offtake, quality control and traceable delivery. Before committing capital, secure an authorised operating route and confirm current rules with the relevant national body. In Ghana, stress-test diesel/power, road access, seasonal working capital and FX; compare solar-assisted drying/storage only against measured utilisation and maintenance costs. Do not underwrite either proposition using stale spot prices or unverified subsidy assumptions.
