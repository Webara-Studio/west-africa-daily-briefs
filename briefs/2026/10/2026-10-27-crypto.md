### Regulation: verify before launch

- **Ghana:** check the Bank of Ghana and Securities and Exchange Commission Ghana for the operative virtual-asset framework, commencement dates, transitional arrangements and which activities require authorisation. Do not treat consultation, a proposed bill or a pilot as a licence. The e-Cedi is a central-bank digital-currency initiative, not a private stablecoin; no current pilot status is asserted here without a dated official update.
- **Nigeria:** check SEC Nigeria and the Central Bank of Nigeria for current VASP registration/approval status, enforcement notices and applicable payment or banking restrictions. A past approval or market announcement should not be assumed to remain valid or cover every product line.
- **Kenya:** consult the Central Bank of Kenya and Capital Markets Authority for current digital-asset rules and implementation notices. Kenya is included as a regional comparator, not a West African market.
- **South Africa:** consult the Financial Sector Conduct Authority and South African Reserve Bank on crypto-asset financial-services licensing, crypto-asset service-provider obligations and payment/stablecoin policy. South Africa is likewise a comparator rather than West Africa.

## Commercial Opportunity

The sharper near-term proposition is **compliant settlement plumbing**, not a speculative coin: serve a defined diaspora or SME payment corridor through appropriately authorised partners, provide a clear quote in both currencies, disclose fees and FX spread, screen counterparties, reconcile transactions end-to-end and pay out through established local rails such as bank transfer or MoMo where permitted.

Start with a tightly scoped partner-led pilot. Measure total delivered cost, settlement time, payout success, fraud/chargeback losses and repeat usage against a conventional remittance provider. Obtain Ghanaian/Nigerian legal advice on licensing, custody, AML/CFT, sanctions, consumer disclosures, tax and data handling before handling customer funds. The principal risks are regulatory perimeter uncertainty, de-pegging/counterparty exposure, FX and liquidity gaps, cybercrime, and confusing an on-chain transfer with lawful local payout.
