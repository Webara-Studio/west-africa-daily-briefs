### IPOs, capital raises and diaspora products

- **Airtel Money:** Multiple 1 October media reports say the company set its London IPO offer price at £1.96 per share and imply a valuation of about £5.3bn. This is a cross-border, London-listed African fintech story—not an IPO on an African exchange. The same-day report of IFC backing for USD90m should be treated as reported until confirmed in primary company/IFC documentation; admission and allocation remain watch items.
- **Other local IPO pipeline:** Search results surfaced no verified offer document or current local African/Caribbean offer opening on 2 October. Ghana’s Kasapreko listing and First Atlantic Bank IPO were earlier 2026 events; Jamaica’s Woodcats Junior Market offer also closed earlier in the year. Jamaica reporting has described Quantas as eyeing a listing, but that is not a scheduled or confirmed IPO.
- **Diaspora investing:** September coverage of Daba described diaspora investment clubs and access to African assets; coverage of global investors accessing Nigeria’s Dangote IPO is also relevant distribution context. These are reported access channels, not proof of a newly launched regulated retail security product. The commercial gap is still a trusted, properly licensed route offering clear custody, fees, FX conversion, tax and liquidity disclosures.

## Commercial Opportunity

The strongest practical angle is **IPO-readiness and diaspora distribution infrastructure**, rather than trying to call short-term market direction. On the issuer side, Ghana and other regional markets need a pipeline of well-governed, investable mid-sized companies: audited accounts, board controls, credible forecasts, investor materials and post-listing reporting. A focused advisory service can sell a staged readiness diagnostic, remediation programme and continuing investor-relations support before underwriting.

On distribution, partner with licensed brokers, custodians and payment providers to make diaspora participation less cumbersome without holding client funds or presenting the product as a guaranteed-return investment. Pilot with one corridor and a small set of listed securities; measure funded-account conversion, all-in FX/custody cost, settlement time, retention and complaint rate. Verify the applicable securities, AML/KYC, marketing and tax rules in both investor and issuer jurisdictions before launch.

## Watch List

- Confirm Airtel Money’s admission date, final prospectus, allocation and the terms/identity of any cornerstone or IFC participation.
- Track GSE issuer announcements and verify the conflicting 1 October GSE index data against the exchange’s official daily report.
- Watch NGX activity after the reported month-end profit-taking and verify high-volume single-name moves against official market reports.
- Follow Jamaica’s Quantas listing intentions and any new TTSE/ECSE offer notices; distinguish an expression of intent from an approved, dated offer.
- Monitor diaspora investment platforms for licensing, custody arrangements and transparent all-in fees—not just headline access claims.
