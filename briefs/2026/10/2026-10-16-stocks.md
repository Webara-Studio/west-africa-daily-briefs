Monitor GSE and NGX for local-currency equity activity; BRVM for the regional West African cross-border market; Nairobi and Johannesburg for larger listed-company and institutional-market signals. In the Caribbean, Jamaica, Trinidad & Tobago and ECSE remain smaller, locally driven markets where individual company announcements can outweigh broad index narratives.

### Diaspora investment products

No newly launched diaspora-focused listed-market product was confirmed. The potential remains real, but a credible offer needs licensed distribution and custody, clear home-country and host-country eligibility, transparent FX conversion and repatriation terms, fee disclosure, investor suitability checks and accessible reporting. A diaspora-branded wrapper without these protections is a trust and compliance liability, not a product advantage.

## Commercial Opportunity

The most interesting near-term angle is a **trusted diaspora investment and issuer-readiness channel**, developed with licensed brokers, custodians and local advisers rather than operating as an unlicensed investment intermediary.

Start with a low-capital information and onboarding layer: verified exchange notices, plain-English issuer and risk explainers, investor education, and introductions to authorised providers. In parallel, offer Ghanaian and regional growth firms a paid readiness package—governance gap assessment, audited-reporting preparation, equity-story development and introductions to qualified advisers. Revenue can come from disclosed research/subscription fees or issuer advisory retainers; do not take transaction-based compensation unless the required permissions are in place.

The commercial test is straightforward: prove repeat diaspora demand, clarify the lawful cross-border distribution route, and secure a regulated partner before building transaction functionality. Given FX, custody and liquidity friction, trust and execution are the product.

## Watch List

- Daily market reports and official price lists from GSE, NGX, BRVM, NSE and JSE; look for turnover and breadth, not just index direction.
- Jamaica Stock Exchange, TTSE and ECSE issuer notices, trading summaries and corporate actions.
- Securities regulator approvals and final prospectuses before describing any offer as an IPO.
- Rights issues, bond programmes and institutional placements that may signal near-term funding demand.
- Any diaspora product's licensing, custody, eligible jurisdictions, FX spread, fees, liquidity and repatriation terms.
