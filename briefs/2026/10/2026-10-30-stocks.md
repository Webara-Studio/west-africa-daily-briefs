## Commercial Opportunity

**The strongest near-term angle is a regulated, disclosure-first diaspora access and intelligence layer, starting with Ghana.** The first product need not be a new fund: an opt-in service could translate official issuer announcements into concise, timestamped alerts, explain basic financial metrics, flag corporate actions and route interested users to licensed brokers. Revenue could come from a disclosed subscription or compliant business-to-business distribution, rather than taking custody of client funds.

A second, more capital-intensive route is arranging SME or sustainable fixed-income issuance through the GSE's existing channels. Start with issuer readiness, audited information, cash-flow analysis and investor demand testing. The main constraints are issuer quality, liquidity, regulatory approval, FX exposure and reliable secondary-market exits. Do not present this as a guaranteed investment opportunity.
