# 📈 IPOs & Stock Market Watch — Friday, 25 September 2026

## Headline Trends

The latest exchange-page observations available in this research run are dated **Thursday, 24 September**, not today’s close. Ghana’s GSE Composite fell 0.50% to 14,215.45, while the BRVM Composite gained 2.97% to 531.63. Nairobi’s All Share rose 0.51% to 248.46; the JSE All Share was down 1.57% to 111,564.58 on its latest displayed observation, dated 23 September. The contrast argues against treating Africa as one uniform risk trade.

NGX news coverage reports the Nigerian benchmark crossing 250,000 points and market capitalisation reaching a record N162.39 trillion earlier this week. Two headline capital-raising stories dominate the pipeline: the planned Dangote Petroleum Refinery offer in Nigeria and Airtel Money’s reported London IPO ambition. Neither should be described as a newly completed IPO on the evidence reviewed.

## Sentiment Snapshot

**Mixed, with a constructive medium-term bias but elevated execution and valuation risk.** The GSE remains strongly positive year to date (+62.09%) despite its latest daily decline; BRVM is also up 53.76% year to date and has recently shown a sharp daily advance. NGX’s record-level headlines signal momentum, but a high nominal index level alone says little about breadth, real returns or foreign-currency performance. The JSE’s latest displayed daily drop is a counterweight. These are dated observations from a market aggregator and press coverage, not a synchronous, audited closing tape across all venues.

## Commercial Opportunity

The most compelling near-term angle is **IPO readiness and investor access infrastructure**, rather than speculating on a headline offer. A Ghana- or Nigeria-focused broker, licensed investment adviser or fintech partner could pilot a diaspora-facing research and onboarding service: issuer-verified deal briefs, KYC and beneficial-owner checks, funding/custody options through regulated partners, allocation-status reporting, and explicit GHS/NGN-to-USD/GBP FX scenarios. Start with a small, opt-in investor cohort and measure verified KYC completion, funded accounts, offer participation and post-listing retention. Do not promise allocations or returns; cross-border securities solicitation, AML/KYC, tax and custody rules require specialist legal review in each target jurisdiction.

For direct portfolio investors, the sharper opportunity is selective exposure to liquid issuers and funds after diligence, not chasing broad index momentum. On the GSE and BRVM, compare valuation, free float, turnover, corporate disclosures and exit capacity; model returns in the investor’s base currency. A 50% local-index gain can still disappoint a foreign investor if FX depreciation, fees or illiquidity absorb the return.

## Watch List

- **Dangote Refinery / NGX:** prospectus, regulator approval, offer dates, investor eligibility and confirmed pricing; press reporting alone is not an actionable offer.
- **Airtel Money:** final venue, share structure, valuation basis and timetable; the reported target is for London, not evidence of a Ghanaian or Nigerian listing.
- **GSE and BRVM:** next official daily reports, market breadth and value traded; test whether recent strength is broadening or concentrated.
- **Caribbean exchanges:** official JSE, TTSE and ECSE notices and market reports before claiming a mover, capital raise or diaspora product.
- **Diaspora access:** any regulated broker, fund or bond product with transparent eligibility, custody, FX conversion and repatriation terms.
