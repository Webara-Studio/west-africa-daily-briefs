The latest cited seasonal material is also not a same-day weather alert: Ghana Meteorological Agency's SON 2026 outlook and FEWS NET's July West Africa seasonal monitor are useful planning references, but not evidence of a new event today. Maintain local monitoring for Gulf of Guinea flood/road disruption and Sahel rainfall stress, particularly where drying, collection and feeder-road access are critical.

## Commercial Opportunity

**Best risk-adjusted angle: a buyer-backed cocoa quality and traceability service in Ghana, starting asset-light.** Enrol a small district cluster through an anchor licensed buyer or processor; provide moisture testing, batch-level records, drying coordination and collection scheduling. Charge for measurable reductions in rejected bags, quality claims or collection delays rather than betting on the bean price. A 60–90 day pilot should track moisture at handover, rejection rate, farmer payment time and buyer collection-to-port dwell time.

For cashew and shea, use the same aggregation discipline only after securing written offtake with grade and delivery terms. Avoid holding inventory against an unverified “market price”.
